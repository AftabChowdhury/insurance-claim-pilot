# DriveSafe Private Auto Policy

## Section 3 – Comprehensive Coverage

### 3.1 Theft of the vehicle
We cover the theft of your insured vehicle, including its permanently installed equipment. If the vehicle is not recovered within 30 days of the reported theft, we treat it as a total loss and settle under Clause 5.1.

### 3.2 Other comprehensive perils
We cover direct physical loss to your insured vehicle caused by fire, vandalism, falling objects, hail, flood, or contact with an animal.

## Section 4 – Collision Coverage

### 4.1 Collision
We cover direct physical loss to your insured vehicle caused by collision with another object or by overturning.

## Section 5 – Settlement

### 5.1 Actual cash value
Total losses are settled at the actual cash value of the vehicle immediately before the loss, based on market value for the same make, model, year, mileage and condition. The amount paid is the actual cash value minus the applicable deductible.

### 5.2 Comprehensive deductible
A deductible of $1,000 applies to each comprehensive loss, including theft.

### 5.3 Limit of liability
The most we will pay for loss to your insured vehicle is the lesser of its actual cash value or $90,000.

## Section 6 – Exclusions

### 6.1 Wear and mechanical failure
We do not cover wear and tear, freezing, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or road damage to tyres.

### 6.2 Unsecured vehicle
If the vehicle is stolen while unlocked with the keys or key fob left in or on the vehicle, we reduce the theft payment by 50 percent.

### 6.3 Personal property
We do not cover personal property in or on the vehicle, such as laptops, phones, bags or clothing. These items may be covered under a homeowners policy.

## Section 8 – Conditions

### 8.4 Police report for theft
For any theft claim you must report the theft to the police within 48 hours of discovering it and provide us with the police report number.

### 8.5 Cooperation
You must cooperate with our investigation, provide all keys and key fobs for the vehicle, and submit to an examination under oath if requested.
