# HomeShield Homeowners Policy

## Section 4 – Covered Perils

### 4.1 Fire and smoke
We cover direct physical loss to the dwelling and personal property caused by fire, lightning or smoke from a sudden and accidental fire. Smoke from fireplaces, agricultural burning or industrial operations is not covered.

### 4.2 Sudden and accidental discharge of water
We cover direct physical loss caused by the sudden and accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, air-conditioning or fire-sprinkler system, or from a household appliance. This includes a burst pipe or a failed washing-machine hose. We also pay the reasonable cost of tearing out and replacing part of the building to reach the system from which the water escaped. We do not pay to repair the system or appliance itself.

### 4.3 Windstorm and hail
We cover direct physical loss caused by windstorm or hail, including damage to roofs, windows and siding. Interior damage from rain or snow is covered only if the wind or hail first creates an opening in the roof or a wall through which the rain or snow enters.

## Section 5 – Deductibles and Limits

### 5.1 Standard deductible
A deductible of $1,000 applies to each covered loss unless another deductible is stated in this section.

### 5.2 Water damage deductible
For losses under Clause 4.2 the deductible is $500 per loss.

### 5.3 Limits of insurance
The most we will pay for one loss is $250,000 for the dwelling and $50,000 for personal property. Payment is made after the deductible is applied.

## Section 7 – Exclusions

### 7.1 Gradual leakage
We do not cover loss caused by continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam over a period of 14 days or more, including rot, mould or deterioration that results from it.

### 7.2 Wear and tear
We do not cover wear and tear, marring, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, latent defect, or loss caused by poor maintenance.

### 7.3 Flood and surface water
We do not cover loss caused by flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or rainwater or groundwater that seeps or leaks through foundations, walls, floors, basements, doors or windows. This exclusion applies regardless of any other cause contributing to the loss.

### 7.4 Intentional loss
We do not cover any loss arising from an act committed by or at the direction of an insured with the intent to cause a loss.

## Section 8 – Conditions

### 8.1 Notice of loss
You must notify us as soon as possible, and no later than 30 days after the loss. Late notice may reduce or void payment if it prevents us from investigating the loss.

### 8.2 Proof of loss
You must provide an itemised list of damaged property, repair estimates or invoices, and photographs when available.

### 8.3 Concealment or fraud
This policy is void if any insured has intentionally concealed or misrepresented any material fact relating to a claim.

## Endorsements

### E-2 Sewer and drain backup
If shown as purchased on your Declarations page, we cover direct physical loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains, up to $10,000 per loss. If this endorsement is not shown on your Declarations page, the exclusion in Clause 7.3 applies to such losses.
