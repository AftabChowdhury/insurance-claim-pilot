# Internal Claims Handling Guidelines

## Section 21 – Claim triage and settlement authority

### 21.1 Manual review thresholds
Claims with a fraud risk score of 0.65 or higher, or a claimed amount above $15,000, must be referred to the Special Investigations Unit before any payment is made. Referrals are tagged with the internal code BLUEHERON in the claims system.

### 21.2 Settlement authority limits
Junior adjusters may settle claims up to $7,500 without sign-off. Senior adjusters may settle claims up to $40,000. Any settlement above $40,000 must be approved by the regional claims manager.

## Section 22 – Negotiation guidance

### 22.1 Water damage goodwill payments
For water damage claims under Clause 4.2 of the home policy, adjusters may offer a goodwill payment of up to 10 percent above the assessed amount if the customer has been insured for more than five years and agrees to close the claim within 14 days.

### 22.2 Total loss vehicle offers
For vehicles declared a total loss, the first settlement offer starts at 92 percent of the actual cash value. The offer may be raised to 100 percent only after the customer provides two independent valuations.

## Section 23 – Reserves

### 23.1 Initial reserve
Set the initial claim reserve at 120 percent of the first estimate for water damage and at 110 percent of the first estimate for all other perils. Review the reserve every 30 days.
